Experimental Polyglot Autonomous Systems Platform

Research-grade UAV simulation,
built to break on purpose.

A non-weaponized UAV test environment studying whether an AI-assisted development agent can construct and maintain a complex distributed autonomous-systems architecture — with an independent safety layer, contracts, provenance, and chaos testing.

Research question

Can an AI-assisted development agent coordinate a heterogeneous autonomous-system architecture spanning approximately 20 programming languages while maintaining correctness, observability, reproducibility, and fault tolerance? If the answer is “no, and here is where it breaks”, that is a result.
